What It Does

The Smartest Way to Hire at Scale

Our AI HR Automation System eliminates the manual, time-consuming process of resume shortlisting. Upload hundreds of resumes — the AI reads, understands, and ranks candidates against your Job Description within seconds.

Built specifically for Indian hiring workflows — supports both Hindi and English resumes, handles diverse education formats (CBSE, ICSE, State Boards), and integrates with Indian job boards like Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed.

  • ✓
    Auto-parse resumes (PDF, DOCX, image scans)
  • ✓
    AI scores each candidate 0–100% against JD
  • ✓
    Ranks candidates — best fit at top
  • ✓
    Auto-sends interview invitations via email/WhatsApp
  • ✓
    Bias-free screening — purely skills & experience
  • ✓

  • ✓
