What It Does

Your Business Data,
Explained in Your Language

Most BI tools are complex and require data analysts. Our AI BI Platform lets any manager, owner, or decision-maker query business data just by typing a question — in Hindi or English — and instantly see a chart or table.

The platform connects to your existing databases (MySQL, PostgreSQL, Excel, Google Sheets, Tally, Zoho), converts your natural language question into an optimized SQL query, runs it, and presents the result visually.
